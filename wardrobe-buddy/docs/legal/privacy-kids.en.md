# Your privacy in Wardrobe Buddy

**The short version: your stuff stays on your phone.**

- **Your clothes, photos, avatar and nickname stay on this phone.** We can't see them. Nobody else can either.
- **A photo of you is up to you.** If you add one to try clothes on, the phone itself finds your shoulders and feet. Nothing is sent anywhere. [D-063: during testing this photo is not blurred; the final rule is decided before release.]
- **Your avatar's height and shape** are pictures you pick. No numbers, nothing about your body is measured.
- **Only photos of clothes,** and only photos of yourself. No friends or family, please.
- **The weather:** if a grown-up says yes, the app asks a weather service for the weather in your town. It sends the town, nothing about you.
- **No ads. No chat. No sharing with others.** The app doesn't follow what you do.
- **A grown-up can save a copy** of your data and photos, for example to keep them on a new phone.
- **A grown-up can delete everything** in the grown-up settings ⚙️. Deleting the app deletes everything too.

Questions? Ask a grown-up to write to [contact email].
