Ludo – Support

The classic board game for 2–4 players: online with friends, on one device, or against the computer.

▶ Play in your browser – works on phones, tablets, computers and TV browsers.

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback? Email cutiermn@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone/iPad model and iOS version.

How to play

  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your base. A 6 also gives you another roll.
  • Move all four tokens around the board and into the centre. You need the exact number to finish.
  • Land on an opponent to send them back to base, except on the star and start squares, which are safe.
  • House rules (extra rolls, three-sixes penalty) can be switched on or off before a game.

Playing online

  • Tap Play online with friends → Create a room, then share the 6-character room code.
  • Friends tap Play online with friends, enter the code and tap Join.
  • The host can give empty seats to computer players (Easy, Medium or Hard) and then starts the game.
  • If your connection drops, reopen the app; the game reconnects automatically. If you are away too long, your turn is played for you so the game keeps going.

Keyboard and TV remote (browser version)

  • Space or Enter: roll the dice, or move the highlighted token.
  • Arrow keys: choose which token to move. 1–4: move that token directly.
  • Tab: move between buttons on the menus.

Troubleshooting

  • Can't connect online: check your internet connection and try again in a minute.
  • Lost a game on one device: unfinished games are saved automatically. Look for Resume game on the main menu.
